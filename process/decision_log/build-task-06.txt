  REAL-DATA EXTENSION: semi-supervised community labelling on SNAP networks with ground-truth communities, solved with the
  existing distributed CG.
  - Graph G (undirected, simple) with Laplacian L = D - A. For each of K chosen communities c, solve (L + a I) x_c = b_c,
    where b_c is the indicator of s seed vertices drawn from community c (fixed seed list, recorded). Label each evaluated
    vertex by argmax_c x_c. Evaluate on vertices that belong to exactly one of the K communities, excluding seeds.
    Accuracy vs ground truth, against simple baselines (majority class; random). Report how accuracy and CG iterations
    depend on a, s and graph sparsity (average degree), and what that says about the data. This is the "conclusions about
    the data" row.
  - L + aI is SPD, so classic CG and pipelined CG are reused unchanged; only the operator changes, from stencil to a
    distributed sparse matrix-vector product (CSR). K right-hand sides: solve them one after another, or as a block if
    that is simple. Time per solve is the unit.
  - Datasets (SNAP, "Networks with ground-truth communities"; the parent checked they are reachable from Vanda):
    email-Eu-core (1,005 / 25,571, department labels), com-Amazon (334,863 / 925,872), com-DBLP (317,080 / 1,049,866),
    com-Youtube (1,134,890 / 2,987,624), com-LiveJournal (3,997,962 / 34,681,189), com-Orkut (3,072,441 / 117,185,083).
    Files: <name>.ungraph.txt.gz plus the .top5000.cmty.txt.gz / .all.cmty.txt.gz community files (check the exact names
    on the SNAP pages). Use the largest K communities in top5000 that are disjoint enough. Explain the choice.

PARALLEL DESIGN TO BUILD AND COMPARE (the grader wants methods compared, communication explained, >=256 cores)
- Distributed CSR, 1D partition of vertices (rows). Compare two partitionings: (a) contiguous blocks of vertex ids with equal
  rows; (b) contiguous blocks with equal nonzeros (degree-balanced). Optionally (c) a cheap reordering (e.g. BFS or
  degree order) to cut ghost count, if time allows. Report the edge cut, ghost-vertex counts and load imbalance per rank.
- Ghost exchange: build send/receive lists once; exchange per SpMV with MPI_Neighbor_alltoallv on a distributed graph
  communicator (MPI-3), or Alltoallv. Time it as t_halo, the same column as the stencil halo, so E5 covers both patterns.
  Optionally overlap the local part of SpMV with the exchange, as for the stencil.
- OpenMP threads in the local SpMV and vector kernels, as now. The GPU offload path is NOT required for graphs.
- Serial scaling (E2 for graphs): the dataset ladder varies vertices and edges together (L12's wording). Plot time per CG
  iteration vs edges and vs vertices, log axes.
- Strong scaling: com-LiveJournal and com-Orkut, 1..288 ranks on Vanda (and up to 96 on Atlas), classic and pipelined.
  Weak scaling stays on synthetic Poisson; say why in the report (a fixed real graph has no size knob; L12: synthetic
  data is the easy way to weak scaling).
